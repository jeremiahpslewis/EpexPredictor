# Eleven-zone outage study: pre-registered table (12 posterior draws)

The pre-registered sample: 312 outage MTUs, six posterior draws from each German fuel-split basin, five headroom
scenarios. Code and records as in `prereg/PREREG_OUTAGE.md` addendum 3, with the hybrid-coupling repairs. All 12 draw
files are complete, with no numerical exceptions.

How to read it:

- **Bracketed ranges.** The brackets labelled "[5%, 95%] across draws" are quantiles over six draws per basin, so
  they are effectively the range across draws.
- **Replay and eta = 1.00.** The two scenarios coincide by construction.
- **Coverage.** Hybrid-coupling MTUs (June–November 2026) are now included. The balanced panel, where all five
  scenarios converged, covers 78.8% of draw-MTUs (54% before the repairs).
- **Known limitation.** At eta = 0.10, 48% of hybrid-coupling draw-MTUs still fail (3% of the others). In the pilot
  draw, these failures had a larger mean replay ΔpDE than the MTUs that converge (+6.08 vs +3.35 EUR/MWh; medians
  +2.21 vs +2.93). Raising the solve limit to 45 s converged 2 of 58. The balanced panel therefore under-represents
  the heavy upper tail at tight headroom.
- **Generic-direction subgroup.** In MTUs that needed the generic inferred direction (about 6% of the balanced panel),
  imports are mechanically held at observed flows. There, German own replacement is 0.64–0.71 and the price rise is
  about twice as large. In mode 0 this subgroup crosses the 30% price threshold (+33%), and in mode 1 it does not
  (+29%). The pooled verdict is the pre-registered one.

Source: `cf11_v2.pkl`. 312 outage MTUs, {'mode0': 6, 'mode1': 6} posterior draws per basin.
