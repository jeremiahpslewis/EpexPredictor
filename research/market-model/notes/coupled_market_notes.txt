Coupled-market model (exp/star.py), state after 2026-11-17. Research harness only; nothing in the repo.

Model: 9 zones (DE + FR NL BE AT PL CZ CH DK). Each zone: supply stack of logistic blocks (low-cost, non-gas A, non-gas B, gas
combined cycle, peakers, curtailment), marginal cost = alpha + heat rate * gas + emission rate * common cost level (one latent
weekly state). German lignite, hard coal, gas and French nuclear and gas capacity follow measured availability (trailing
14-day maximum of realised output, one day late). German must-run floors per fuel. Pumped storage: P * tanh((price - day
mean) / kappa). Exchange Germany -> neighbour: -Ci + (Ce + Ci) * sigmoid((price difference - m) / kappa). Hour/day-type shift
of German demand. Full-support priors (lognormal capacities, normal gas links); capacity centres from realised output.
Likelihood (version 9): generative = shocks to every part -> clearing of all zones -> observed prices and quantities.
  Terms: German output by fuel, pumped storage, scheduled exchange per border (uncoupled hours), balance of each zone in GW at
  the observed price, and the Jacobian of the clearing system (closed form; hours with equal prices treated as one market).
  No price-space fitting; the price is what the estimated parts imply when the market is cleared (nested bisection).

What each check showed (fit date 2026-09-18, 540 days; second date 2026-02-02):
  v1 prices only:                 price MAE 1.43/1.34 ct; exchange capacities < 1 GW (data 2-3.5), a 29 GW fictitious peaker, lignite at half its level.
  prior predictive, first priors: simulated markets too cheap above 10 GW, no floor at zero (installed power is not available power).
  prior predictive, corrected:    floor at zero, quantities in realised ranges; data at the upper edge of the prior band.
  v3 quantities as data:          parts match; price MAE 1.60; 34 GW catch-all block of width 97.
  energy balance by source:       price response of German supply = net imports 18 GW, pumped storage 9.8, lignite 7.9, gas 6.2, hard coal 4.0; rest constant.
  v4 + pumped storage:            storage fits (corr 0.88); catch-all unchanged -> not the cause.
  v5 balance in quantity space:   catch-all gone (12.9 GW must-run); cleared price 12 ct too high above 45 GW (simultaneity).
  v6 gas availability measured:   gas output corr 0.84 -> 0.91; winter shortage smaller, not gone.
  v7 + Jacobian:                  bias by load mostly gone; a Polish block diverged (neighbours' terms were down-weighted).
  v8 coherent weights:            price MAE 1.43/1.30; bias by load within 0.5 ct; two starts agree; exchange functions degenerate to steps.
  v9 coupled hours as one market: price MAE 1.44/1.30; bias by load within 0.2 ct (0.7 at the top on the winter date); net exports -10.4..+8.7 vs -10.8..+10.0 (corr 0.89).
Open misfits: model 1.2-2.2 ct too low in the high-gas months of 2026; 0.4-0.8 ct too low at 16-24 h; Dutch exchange curve too large and steep;
  Nordic and Luxembourg borders absent; simulate-and-refit check not yet run on this version; no forecasts made with it yet.
For comparison, in-sample German price MAE of the free-form curve: 1.31/1.24 ct (no neighbours), 1.20/1.17 (neighbours as a shift).
